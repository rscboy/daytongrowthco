# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 12, 2026

### Introduction

DaytonGrowthCo. LLC ("we," "us," or "our") explains here how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, request a free mini audit or website concept, or engage our services. This policy applies to our websites and related communications, not to third-party sites or services that have their own policies.

### Information We Collect

**Information You Provide:** When you fill out a form, request an audit or website concept, communicate with us, or become a client, we may collect your name, business name, business email, phone number (if provided), team size, website address, service interests, workflow details, project materials, and other information you choose to provide.

**Automatically Collected Information:** When you visit our website, our service providers may collect device, browser, IP-address, operating-system, page, interaction, referring-site, performance, and approximate-location information through cookies, analytics, session tools, and similar technologies.

**Client-Service Information:** If you engage us, we may process information reasonably needed to perform agreed services, such as website content, brand materials, approved credentials, customer-workflow examples, call scripts, pricing rules, project records, and communications. We ask clients not to provide information that is unnecessary for the agreed scope.

### Business-Email Personalization and Favicon Preview

If you enter a business email in our personalization module or contact form, the website may derive the domain from that email in your browser to personalize the page and to request a favicon from a public web location or public icon source. The favicon is shown only as a temporary interface preview beside the field.

We do not treat the favicon as a marketing asset, sell it, license it, claim ownership of it, or use it for advertising, profiling, or business promotion. We do not describe a publicly accessible favicon as public domain; the rights in it remain with its owner. The preview does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation. The browser may make a direct request to the relevant domain, DuckDuckGo’s icon service, or Google’s favicon service; those providers may process that request under their own policies. If an icon cannot be found, the preview is hidden.
