# The Better Quote Program™ | DaytonGrowthCo.

> Upload an expensive written service quote. A real person shops it with legitimate local providers for a better qualifying, comparable option.

The Better Quote Program™ is a human-led quote-shopping and consumer-advocacy service. It is not an automated comparison engine, contractor directory, lead-generation marketplace, coupon site, or a promise to find the absolute cheapest provider.

## How it works

1. Send your written quote or estimate.
2. Real people review it and contact local providers.
3. DaytonGrowthCo reports whether a qualifying lower comparable quote was found, typically within about 48 business hours after receiving required information.
4. If qualifying savings are found, the customer sees the savings and the disclosed success fee before paying. If no qualifying savings are found, no success fee is due.

## Pricing

There is no upfront search fee. The success fee is tied to verified qualifying savings and is explained before the search begins. See the current schedule: https://www.daytongrowth.co/quote/pricing/
