3 Business-Email Personalization and Favicon Preview

If you enter a business email in our personalization module or contact form, the website may derive the domain from that email in your browser to personalize the page and to request a favicon from a public web location or public icon source. The favicon is shown only as a temporary interface preview beside the field.

We do not treat the favicon as a marketing asset, sell it, license it, claim ownership of it, or use it for advertising, profiling, or business promotion. We do not describe a publicly accessible favicon as public domain; the rights in it remain with its owner. The preview does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation. The browser may make a direct request to the relevant domain, DuckDuckGo’s icon service, or Google’s favicon service; those providers may process that request under their own policies. If an icon cannot be found, the preview is hidden.
